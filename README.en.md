## Use

Copy an entire `skills/<name>/` folder into a Skill directory supported by your host, or ask an assistant to read its `SKILL.md`. Installation paths and invocation depend on the host. Individual READMEs include synthetic examples and check commands.

No API key is needed for this repository. It includes no connector and does not automatically publish content or call paid services. Users provide authorized inputs and tool access.

## Validation limits

Local structure, links and synthetic fixtures were checked. Video direction passed 13 tests; weekly review, reference adaptation and dashboard design passed 4 each. The writing package checked exact membership, 14 links and 2 synthetic cases. Model quality, live platforms, business outcomes and browser visuals remain unverified.

All examples are independently synthetic. `python3 -B scripts/generate_public_examples.py` reproduces the public examples without reading business files or accessing a network.
