# Qianchuan Script Creator

By the creator of [FateClear — a free birthday charting and interpretation website](https://fateclear.com) · [Projects and updates on X](https://x.com/liu_xiaoyu27334)

Turn supplied product facts and production constraints into shootable short-video scripts. Supports livestream and product-page destinations, targeted revisions, reviews, single-variable variants, and data-informed testing hypotheses.

This is a separately prepared general public edition. It retains the author's task-routing and fact/destination-checking purposes, with new intake templates, delivery tables, and synthetic cases. It excludes the private course knowledge base and does not claim equivalent methods or results. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Douyin, Qianchuan, or the source-course creators.

## Preview the handoff

The [synthetic cases](examples/synthetic-cases.md) plan a 15-second video script using the supplied tabletop setup, stand and phone, then vary only the opening shot. Product facts and unconfirmed promotions stay separate. A script and testing hypothesis do not demonstrate platform approval or campaign results.

**Shortest entry:** ask a Markdown-capable assistant to read this folder's [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), then supply an example or your own authorized input. Keep the whole folder; setup details follow below.

This README is available in three languages. Operational instructions, templates and some examples remain primarily Chinese. You may request an output language, but cross-host invocation and Japanese generation quality have not been validated.

## Use

Place this whole folder in a host that supports Markdown skills and enable it according to that host's instructions, or ask an assistant to read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). The operational instructions and templates are in Chinese. Example request:

> Use qianchuan-script-creator to draft a 15-second product video from my supplied facts. Only a tabletop and the product are available. Add no unverified promotion, and provide one variant that changes only the opening shot.

Supply product facts, audience/context, destination, duration, and production constraints. For performance analysis, include metric definitions and observation windows. Do not include account secrets or customer-level records.
