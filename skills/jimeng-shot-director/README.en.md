# Shot Planning and Footage Review

[中文](README.md) · By xiaoyu312: [FateClear](https://fateclear.com), a free birthday charting and interpretation website · [X](https://x.com/liu_xiaoyu27334)

Turn a video-shot brief into an action plan, reference responsibilities, a usable prompt and a footage review checklist. For video creators, storyboard planners and teams working with generative video.

This is an independent community workflow. The legacy folder name `jimeng-shot-director` preserves invocation compatibility; it does not imply endorsement by a platform or any author named in the historical provenance notice.

## Use

Copy the complete folder into the Skills directory supported by your assistant and follow its invocation instructions. Alternatively, ask your assistant to read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) before giving it a shot task. No installation script, API key or environment configuration is needed.

Example request:

> Use $jimeng-shot-director with examples/brief.json. Plan one four-second shot of an unbranded cardboard box opening. Deliver text only, and identify missing assets and assumptions.

Use the [shot working card](assets/shot-card.md) when a structured handoff helps. Proposed parameters are not verified platform capabilities. A text-planning request does not authorize generation submission.

## Included scope

- Task selection for new shots, continuations, local edits, transitions and simple spatial previs.
- Reference responsibilities, action/camera relationships, continuity and distinct time bases.
- Separate prompts and production notes, with issue-by-issue review of footage actually provided.

The working card and synthetic exercise were newly authored for this public edition. It contains no manual snapshots, case-study prompts, node-by-node study notes, media, private brand materials or execution history. It does not promise the private predecessor's complete methodology or results.

## Example and validation limits

[brief.json](examples/brief.json) is explicitly labeled, independently constructed synthetic text, not anonymized client data. No generated video is bundled, and the exercise does not demonstrate platform output quality.

Release preparation checks cover skill metadata, package-local links, synthetic input and instruction-following scenarios. Host installation, live generation platforms, video quality and business impact have not been verified. Confirm current platform capabilities and inspect actual returned footage during use.
