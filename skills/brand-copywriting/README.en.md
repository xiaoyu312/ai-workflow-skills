# Brand Copywriting

[中文](README.md) · [FateClear: free birthday charting and interpretation](https://fateclear.com) · [Author on X](https://x.com/liu_xiaoyu27334)

A general AI skill for turning supplied product facts into copy for a defined audience and channel. It supports product and operations teams, content creators, and copy collaborators who need consistent claims.

## Capabilities

- Route requests for names, headlines, short lines, product pages, packaging, spoken copy, and diagnosis.
- Separate supported facts, editorial choices, and unsubstantiated claims.
- Produce new copy, targeted revisions, or a diagnosis while preserving relevant limitations and evidence.
- Use original blank templates and a fully synthetic example without a private knowledge base.

This public edition retains the general purpose and user-authored task routing, with independently written workflow instructions and templates. It omits the private edition's course knowledge base, lesson map, and source materials. It does not promise to reproduce the original course methodology or results.

## Use

Place the complete `brand-copywriting` folder in a host's supported skill directory, or ask an assistant to read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Host setup varies. This package installs no dependencies, makes no network requests, and does not publish copy automatically. The operational instructions and templates are in Chinese.

Example request:

> Use brand-copywriting to draft a product description from the specifications I provide. Preserve relevant limitations and do not invent sales figures, benefits, or offers. Give a recommended version and one alternative with a different tone.

- [Copy brief](assets/copy-brief.md)
- [Delivery sheet](assets/delivery-sheet.md)
- [Synthetic example](examples/synthetic-product.md)

Skill structure, local references, and the example's claim-to-fact mapping have been checked. Host installation, model performance, and sales outcomes remain unverified.
